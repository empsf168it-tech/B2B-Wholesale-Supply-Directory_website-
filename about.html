Built for the Next Generation.

THREAD was founded to solve a critical industry challenge: the fragmented, opaque, and outdated nature of apparel wholesale sourcing. Today, we empower over 50,000 fashion retailers, boutique owners, and enterprise fashion houses to discover, evaluate, and trade with world-class manufacturers in over 30 countries.

The Mission

To build the world's most transparent, reliable, and sustainable B2B fashion supply network—accelerating commerce while championing ethical garment production.
